<span style="color:#4eb24e;">Champagne：</span><br>

Commander. I have always dreamt of a time like this.<br>

Velvety purple juice foams up, a sweet fragrance rising up as the juices get squeezed out. Her hands grow warmer with the work.<br>

<br>

<span style="color:#4eb24e;">Champagne：</span><br>
I will be looking forward to the day we finally get to enjoy what we created together.<br>
