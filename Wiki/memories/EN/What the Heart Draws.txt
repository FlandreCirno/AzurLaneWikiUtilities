1 Soulless Art<br>
Port – Office<br>
As I focus on work, from the corner of my eye I see something whir by the window.<br>
I look up and my gaze meets someone hiding behind the windowsill.<br>
<span style="color:#4eb24e;">Raffaello：</span><br>
Eep...!<br>
<br>
Her emerald hair sways as she hides skittishly like a tiny animal.<br>
<span style="color:#3498DB;" class="shikikanname">指挥官</span>：<br>
...Raffaello?<br>
<span style="color:#4eb24e;">Raffaello：</span><br>
...Meow, meow!<br>
<span style="color:#3498DB;" class="shikikanname">指挥官</span>：<br>
Are you trying to fool me by pretending to be a cat?<br>
<span style="color:#4eb24e;">Raffaello：</span><br>
Ehehehe... You got me~☆<br>
<br>
Raffaello peeks out her head and enters my office confidently, her usual sketchbook in hand.<br>
<span style="color:#3498DB;" class="shikikanname">指挥官</span>：<br>
What were you observing today? The ants crawling across windowsill? The birds flying by?<br>
<span style="color:#4eb24e;">Raffaello：</span><br>
Oh, no no no. I'm here to draw on my favorite source of inspiration! But...<br>
<br>
Raffaello uncharacteristically trails off and hesitates, her expression becoming somewhat stiff.<br>
<span style="color:#3498DB;" class="shikikanname">指挥官</span>：<br>
But?<br>
<br>
She sits on the sofa, fidgeting, and flips her sketchbook open.<br>
<span style="color:#4eb24e;">Raffaello：</span><br>
Look, Commander, this is what I've been drawing recently.<br>
<br>
She hands me the sketchbook, and all its sketches are of a figure working, napping, dining...<br>
<span style="color:#3498DB;" class="shikikanname">指挥官</span>：<br>
They're all drawings of... me?<br>
<span style="color:#4eb24e;">Raffaello：</span><br>
That's right! Over the last few days, I've been observing you in various locations and from different angles.<br>
The dining hall, the office, when you were walking across the port... Because right now, you're my most important observation subject~♪<br>
<span style="color:#3498DB;" class="shikikanname">指挥官</span>：<br>
...I never even noticed.<br>
<span style="color:#4eb24e;">Raffaello：</span><br>
Heheh, that's the bread-and-butter of every good observer~ But here's the problem...<br>
<br>
Raffaello points at her sketchbook.<br>
<span style="color:#4eb24e;">Raffaello：</span><br>
See? This! I drew your profile while you were sorting documents; the lines are accurate, the shading is precise...<br>
But this sketch doesn't have one iota of the real thing's charm... No matter how similar it might appear superficially, it'll never be more than a soulless drawing!<br>
<span style="color:#3498DB;" class="shikikanname">指挥官</span>：<br>
You've spent days working on this?<br>
<span style="color:#4eb24e;">Raffaello：</span><br>
Yeah! I want to draw you with more emotion, Commander!<br>
I don't want to just capture what's on the surface. That spark in your eyes when you're focused, the way your eyelashes droop when you're tired, the way your lips curl when you smile...<br>
I can see it all, but I just can't put it down onto paper... I can't capture what's hidden beneath the surface...<br>
I'm so stumped! Commander, come up with a solution~☆<br>
<span style="color:#3498DB;" class="shikikanname">指挥官</span>：<br>
I'm no artist, so I'm not sure I can give you anything useful off the cuff...<br>
<span style="color:#4eb24e;">Raffaello：</span><br>
There are a lot more drawings, so could you come to my room to take a look? I think you'll understand what I mean when you see them.<br>
<span style="color:#3498DB;" class="shikikanname">指挥官</span>：<br>
Right now?<br>
<span style="color:#4eb24e;">Raffaello：</span><br>
Of course♪ It's about time you take a break, too! And the drawings I have in my room are much more... substantial than the ones in this sketchbook.<br>
<span style="color:#3498DB;" class="shikikanname">指挥官</span>：<br>
Substantial?<br>
<span style="color:#4eb24e;">Raffaello：</span><br>
Well, you'll see when we get there~☆<br>
<br>
Raffaello pulls me along half-forcibly, her figure running just ahead, bathed in sunlight.<br>

<span style="color:#4eb24e;">Raffaello：</span><br>
There's still such a wide gap between what I feel and what I end up drawing...<br>
When you looked at me earlier, my heart skipped a beat. But when I tried to sketch it, what came out felt like some lifeless emotion.<br>
<span style="color:#3498DB;" class="shikikanname">指挥官</span>：<br>

<span style="color:#4eb24e;">Raffaello：</span><br>
No... What's wrong is that it's not capturing that "heart-throbbing" feeling I got from looking at you earlier.<br>
<br>
The room settles into silence, save for the occasional rustling of pages caressed by the wind blowing in through the window.<br>
<span style="color:#4eb24e;">Raffaello：</span><br>
I see...! I get it now! I really am a genius~☆<br>
<br>
After a few seconds, Raffaello suddenly shoots to her feet, her eyes sparkling.<br>
<span style="color:#4eb24e;">Raffaello：</span><br>
No wonder I can't draw it! All I've been doing is observing you, but I never actually experienced what it's like to be with you!<br>
If I experience more of these heart-throbbing moments with you...<br>
If I spend more time with you and immerse myself deeper in these emotions, that'll solve my problem!<br>
<span style="color:#3498DB;" class="shikikanname">指挥官</span>：<br>
So what, exactly, are we going to do?<br>

You and I are going to go out and have fun, so I experience more feelings with you♪ So my heart skips a beat lots of times♪<br>
You said you'd help me out earlier, so no backsies!<br>
<br>
Raffaello holds my hand tightly and pulls me along.<br>

We come to a small sweets store in the port's shopping district.<br>

<span style="color:#4eb24e;">Raffaello：</span><br>
Tadah! This is one of my secret bases, the Dessert Gallery!<br>
<span style="color:#3498DB;" class="shikikanname">指挥官</span>：<br>
These are desserts?<br>
<span style="color:#4eb24e;">Raffaello：</span><br>

<br>
Raffaello points at a color palette cake in the display case.<br>
<span style="color:#4eb24e;">Raffaello：</span><br>
The white part is cream cheese, the red is strawberry jam, the purple is blueberry jam, the yellow is mango...<br>
Each "paint" tastes different! It's a palette that makes you choose the right color to taste!<br>
<span style="color:#3498DB;" class="shikikanname">指挥官</span>：<br>
I think I'm starting to understand why you brought me here...<br>
<span style="color:#4eb24e;">Raffaello：</span><br>
That's right! To make sure my heart skips a beat like I want, I have to bring you to my favorite places!<br>
<br>
I watch Raffaello taste the cake, a sight that's all the more brilliant under the sunlight.<br>
<span style="color:#4eb24e;">Raffaello：</span><br>
...You know, I always eat these desserts, so why do they taste and look extra good today?<br>
<span style="color:#3498DB;" class="shikikanname">指挥官</span>：<br>
Hm?<br>
<br>
Raffaello lifts a piece of the blue cake on her fork and holds it up to the sun.<br>
<span style="color:#4eb24e;">Raffaello：</span><br>
Doesn't this blue look a lot like today's sky? It's such a pretty azure, but it has this milky white glow when the light hits it...<br>
Blue's supposed to feel cool, but it looks so warm...<br>
<span style="color:#3498DB;" class="shikikanname">指挥官</span>：<br>
Maybe because it's a nice day?<br>
<span style="color:#4eb24e;">Raffaello：</span><br>
No, that's not why! It feels warmer because I'm with you, dummy!<br>
But this isn't enough. My heart didn't throb the right way yet! Hmmm... how do I make it happen again...?<br>
<span style="color:#3498DB;" class="shikikanname">指挥官</span>：<br>
Where do I come in to this?<br>
<span style="color:#4eb24e;">Raffaello：</span><br>
Feed me please~☆<br>
<br>
Faced with her direct demand, I have no option but to feed her the blue cake.<br>
She takes a bite, puffing up her cheeks with a satisfied smile as she tastes the cake.<br>
<span style="color:#4eb24e;">Raffaello：</span><br>
Mmm, delish! I've been drawing for so long I've gotten pretty hungry!<br>
<span style="color:#3498DB;" class="shikikanname">指挥官</span>：<br>
You don't have to eat it so fast.<br>
<br>
As soon as she's finished eating, I go to pick up another piece, only for Raffaello to snatch the fork out of my hand and point it at me.<br>
<span style="color:#4eb24e;">Raffaello：</span><br>
I can't be eating everything, you know. Here, have some too, Commander! Say "ahhhn"~<br>
<span style="color:#3498DB;" class="shikikanname">指挥官</span>：<br>
Ahhhn...<br>
<br>
Beneath the sheer weight of her expectant gaze, I obediently open wide.<br>
<span style="color:#4eb24e;">Raffaello：</span><br>
How is it?<br>
<span style="color:#3498DB;" class="shikikanname">指挥官</span>：<br>
Hmm... nice and sweet.<br>
<span style="color:#4eb24e;">Raffaello：</span><br>
Ehehehe♪<br>
<br>
Raffaello grins contentedly and shifts her attention to the red cake this time.<br>
<span style="color:#4eb24e;">Raffaello：</span><br>
Mmhhh, scrumptious! You give it a try too, Commander!<br>
It's sooo delicious! Here, don't keep me waiting! Open wide~<br>
<br>
Her smile is so innocent and pure-hearted that the thought of refusing never once crosses my mind.<br>
<span style="color:#3498DB;" class="shikikanname">指挥官</span>：<br>
Mm... You're right. It really is good.<br>
<span style="color:#4eb24e;">Raffaello：</span><br>
See? I told you~♪<br>
<br>
Suddenly, Raffaello's satisfied smile turns into a bright red blush. She covers her face with her hands and peeks at me through her fingers.<br>
<span style="color:#3498DB;" class="shikikanname">指挥官</span>：<br>
...What's wrong? Why are you acting bashful all of a sudden?<br>
<span style="color:#4eb24e;">Raffaello：</span><br>
Uuuuu! My heart just skipped a beat! A real badump!<br>
<span style="color:#3498DB;" class="shikikanname">指挥官</span>：<br>
Do you still want some?<br>
<span style="color:#4eb24e;">Raffaello：</span><br>
Hmm... Let me keep feeding you for now... I want to savor this feeling a little longer!<br>
<br>
Raffaello gently offers me more cake.<br>
